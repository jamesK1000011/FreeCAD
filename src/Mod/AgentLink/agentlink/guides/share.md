# Share: library packs

- Ask which licence before `library_pack`; don't choose one for the person. CC-BY-4.0 and CC-BY-SA-4.0 need the name to credit; MIT needs the copyright holder; CC0 gives up all rights.
- List every remixed part in `credits` with its licence. Packs are refused when a credited part's licence forbids the one chosen (no-derivatives, non-commercial, share-alike): say why, and offer the licence that works.
- The STEP and STL headers carry only the file's name and the author given. Check `personal` in the answer; if it is there, don't share the pack until it is fixed.
- Uploading the pack anywhere is the person's to do.
